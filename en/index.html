01About

After graduating from the Faculty of Economics at the University of Tokyo, I earned a master's degree from the Graduate School of Interdisciplinary Information Studies at the same university. As an undergraduate, I studied microeconomics and game theory in the Matsui and Oyama seminars. In graduate school, I conducted research on smart cities in the Koshizuka Laboratory.

After completing my master's degree, I joined the workforce and worked as an infrastructure engineer at LY Corporation (formerly Yahoo Japan Corporation) and as a software engineer at Atrae, Inc.

Since 2026, I have returned to the University of Tokyo as a project researcher and research assistant, while pursuing admission to the doctoral program.

Research Interests

Horizontal: economics to information science. Vertical: theory to implementation.
